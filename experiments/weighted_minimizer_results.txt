Ordinary vs Weighted Minimizer Results
Dataset: E. coli vs Shigella
k=15, w=10

Accuracy:
Exact Jaccard:              0.539657
Ordinary minimizer Jaccard: 0.527781
Ordinary error:             0.011876
Weighted minimizer Jaccard: 0.520648
Weighted error:             0.019010

Benchmark (E. coli):
Ordinary minimizers:  816,575
Ordinary runtime:     15.0887 seconds
Weighted minimizers:  844,940
Weighted runtime:     15.1337 seconds

Conclusion:
The simple complexity-based weighting changed minimizer selection
but did not improve Jaccard accuracy or runtime on this dataset.
